# CCS 101 · Topic-linked question bank

Answer from memory, using examples where possible. Then use the [answer guide](ANSWER_KEY.md) and log a concrete action in the [tracker](../PROGRESS_TRACKER.md).

## 01 · Fundamentals of communication
### C01-Q1
Draw or describe the communication process and explain the role of feedback and context.
### C01-Q2
Distinguish message from channel, using an email example.
### C01-Q3
Rewrite “Send me that thing soon” as a clear, complete, courteous request. Explain two changes.

## 02 · Collaboration, listening, oral and reading skills
### C02-Q1
Distinguish hearing from listening and outline four moves for active listening.
### C02-Q2
A group has duplicated work and missed a deadline. Propose a practical collaboration plan.
### C02-Q3
Distinguish skimming, scanning, and close reading; state when each is useful.

## 03 · Study skills and information literacy
### C03-Q1
Reproduce SQ3R and explain how recall differs from rereading.
### C03-Q2
Give a realistic three-session revision plan that includes retrieval practice and delayed review.
### C03-Q3
Evaluate a source using author, evidence, relevance, currency, and purpose; explain how you would cite it.

## 04 · Writing skills
### C04-Q1
Distinguish revising, editing, and proofreading.
### C04-Q2
Write a paragraph plan with a topic sentence, support, and explanation for a claim about active study.
### C04-Q3
Explain how to paraphrase and cite an idea without patchwriting.
